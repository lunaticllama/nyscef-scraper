NYSCEF Document Scraper

A browser bookmarklet that extracts the document list from any NYSCEF case page — no login required, works wherever you can open a browser.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+B  |  Edge: Ctrl+Shift+B  |  Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+B
  2. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

    Drag the button below up to your bookmarks bar

    📄 NYSCEF Scraper

    Can't drag? Right-click the button → "Bookmark this link"

  3. Log in to NYSCEF normally Go to iapps.courts.state.ny.us/nyscef/Login and sign in with your credentials.
  4. Navigate to a case's Document List Click My Cases/Appeals → click a case number → make sure the Document List tab is selected.
  5. Click the bookmark A panel appears with all documents listed. Sort by any column or copy the full list as CSV.

Note: The bookmarklet always loads the latest version from this server, so updates are automatic. Click it again on any NYSCEF page to close the panel.
